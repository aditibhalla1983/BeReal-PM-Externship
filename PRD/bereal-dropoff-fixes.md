# What features could solve: forgetting to post, post-boredom, and rarely checking friends?

These are three distinct engagement failures, not one problem:

- **Forget to post** → the notification/trigger isn't strong or timely enough
- **Bored after posting** → the app has nothing left to offer once the daily obligation is done
- **Rarely check friends' BeReals** → there's no pull back into the app between posts

Each needs a different fix — a blanket "post more" push won't solve all three.

## The 3 I would explore most

1. **Post-Then-Explore** → gives the moment right after posting a reason to stay
2. **Passive Presence Widget** → pulls friend activity outside the app, so checking in doesn't depend on remembering to open it
3. **Context-Aware Nudge** → fixes the forgetting problem with existing data, no new infrastructure

They map directly onto the three failure points:

**Forget → Bored → Rarely check** becomes **Smarter trigger → Extended moment → Ambient visibility**

## One strategic principle

I'd define the product goal as:

> Fix each drop-off point with the smallest possible addition — a smarter trigger, a few extra seconds of purpose after posting, and a passive way to stay aware of friends — rather than solving all three with one heavier feature like a feed or algorithm.

This matters because the three problems have different root causes. Bundling them into one big fix (e.g., "add a content feed to fix boredom and friend-checking at once") would solve engagement at the cost of the minimalism that makes BeReal itself. The right response is several small, targeted nudges, not one bigger surface.

## My strongest PM concept

I'd combine **Post-Then-Explore + Passive Presence Widget**:

**The Moment Doesn't End at Posting:** Posting is no longer a dead end — it opens a short, optional next step (a friend pairing, a question, a reply prompt). And outside the app, a home-screen widget keeps one close friend's latest moment quietly visible, so remembering friends posted doesn't require remembering to open BeReal at all.

That creates:

**Post → Small optional extension (not boredom) → Friend visible passively (not forgotten) → Natural return tomorrow**

It fits BeReal's identity because nothing here asks users to produce more content or check a feed — it just makes the single daily moment slightly more social immediately after it happens, and makes friends' existing moments visible without effort. The fix for all three drop-off points is *less friction*, not *more content*.
