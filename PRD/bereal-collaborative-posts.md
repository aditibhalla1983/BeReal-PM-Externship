# What if BeReal allowed collaborative posts?

If BeReal introduced collaborative posts, the goal would be to boost **authentic engagement between the people who already know each other** — not to open a public co-creation feature like Duets or collabs on other platforms. Collaboration should still be spontaneous, unedited, and limited to real relationships, not a content-production tool.

## 10 feature ideas

| # | Feature idea | How it works | How it boosts authentic engagement | Why it still feels like BeReal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Same Moment** | When two friends post within the same few minutes, the app offers to pair their two unedited photos into one shared post. | Turns a coincidence into a shared social object friends will react to and talk about. | Both photos stay raw and untouched — it's a pairing, not an edit. |
| 2 | **Group BeReal** | Friends physically together can trigger one shutter moment that captures everyone's front/back camera at once. | Rewards being together in real life, which is BeReal's core value, with a richer shared artifact than a solo post. | Still one shot, no retakes, no live audience — just a bigger single moment. |
| 3 | **Add-a-Friend** | After posting, a user can tag one friend who was physically with them; that friend's own BeReal (if posted) links to the same moment. | Creates a reason to check whether the people you were with also posted, prompting mutual engagement. | Uses existing friend posts — no new content is generated. |
| 4 | **Shared Caption** | Two tagged friends can jointly edit one caption under a linked pair of posts, visible only to their mutual friends. | Gives friends a lightweight, low-effort way to co-narrate a moment together. | Text-only, private to the friend group — not a performance for followers. |
| 5 | **RealGroups Post** | Small private groups (max ~8) get an optional shared board where any member's BeReal from that day auto-appears. | Encourages group check-ins beyond one-on-one friendships, especially for trip/roommate/team groups. | Sharing stays capped to people users actually chose into the group. |
| 6 | **Reaction Relay** | A friend can respond to your BeReal with their own live BeReal instead of a comment, creating a linked mini-thread. | Converts a passive reaction into a second authentic moment, deepening the exchange. | Still one unedited shot per response — no retakes, no filters. |
| 7 | **Together Streak** | Track how many times two friends have appeared in the same collaborative post (Same Moment or Group BeReal), shown privately to just the two of them. | Rewards recurring real-life togetherness rather than solo posting frequency. | The reward is shared history, not a public leaderboard or score. |
| 8 | **Moment Request** | A user can ping a friend: "Post with me right now" — both get a synced capture window for the next few minutes. | Creates a spontaneous, mutual reason to open the app together, right now. | No scheduling or planning — still real-time and unscripted. |
| 9 | **Double Take** | If a friend reacts to your BeReal with a specific RealMoji, they're prompted to optionally attach their own current BeReal as a reply. | Makes reacting slightly richer without adding pressure — entirely optional. | Keeps the existing lightweight reaction mechanic; collaboration is opt-in, not required. |
| 10 | **Event Circle** | For a planned hangout (birthday, trip, game night), attendees can opt into a shared circle where all their BeReals from that day/event link together automatically. | Gives a natural reason for a whole friend group to post and revisit the same day together. | Limited to a specific real event, not an ongoing public collab feature. |

## The 3 I would explore most

1. **Same Moment** → turns coincidence into connection
2. **Group BeReal** → rewards real-life togetherness directly
3. **Moment Request** → creates spontaneous, mutual real-time engagement

They solve three different angles of "collaboration":

**Coincidental → Physical → Intentional**

None of them require an algorithmic feed or public co-creation tools — they all stay inside the existing friend graph and one-shot capture model.

## One strategic principle

I'd define the product goal as:

> Turn moments that already involve more than one person into shared artifacts, instead of separate, disconnected posts.

This matters because BeReal's authenticity comes from the *unedited single shot* — collaborative posting can't become a joint-editing or content-production tool without undermining that. The right collaborative features are the ones that capture something that was already happening between real people, not ones that let people build something together after the fact.

## My strongest PM concept

I'd combine **Same Moment + Together Streak**:

**Shared Moments:** When two friends' BeReals land close together in time, the app quietly pairs them and — over time — shows the pair a private count of how many "Same Moments" they've shared.

Example:

> *"You and Priya have shared 6 Same Moments"*
> [paired photo from today]

That creates:

**Post → Coincidental overlap → Paired moment → Private shared history → Reason to post together again**

It fits BeReal's low-pressure identity because the collaboration is **discovered, not requested** — nobody has to plan or perform anything. It simply rewards the real-life overlap that was already happening, and turns it into something worth returning for.
