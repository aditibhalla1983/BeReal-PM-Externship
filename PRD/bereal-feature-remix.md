# 10 Feature Remixes for BeReal
### Borrowed mechanics from Instagram, Snapchat & TikTok — reworked for authenticity, minimalism, and MAU growth

Each remix follows one rule: **the original feature's growth mechanic is kept, but its curation/performance mechanic is stripped out.** BeReal's edge is that nothing is polished, scheduled, or gamed — so every feature below is filtered through "does this still work if the content can't be curated?"

---

## 1. Stories → **"Replays"** (from Instagram/Snapchat Stories)

- **Original mechanic:** Ephemeral, sequential content that drives daily opens and a public "who's watching me" performance loop.
- **Remix:** Friends can request a *Replay* — a no-notice, second unscheduled BeReal within the same day, capped at one per person per day. No curation window, no multi-slide story editing, no filters.
- **Why it fits:** Keeps the "check throughout the day" habit loop without turning the feed into a highlight reel.
- **MAU impact:** Adds a second daily touchpoint tied to social reciprocity (someone requested *you* specifically), which is a stronger reopen trigger than a passive notification.

## 2. Streaks → **"Honesty Streaks"** (from Snapchat)

- **Original mechanic:** Consecutive-day posting streak between two users; famously addictive but also a source of guilt/pressure and streak-restoration scams.
- **Remix:** Streaks track mutual *authentic* posting — both people posting within their actual notification window, not just posting *something*. Missing a day doesn't reset to zero; it just pauses, and streaks are private between the two friends (never a public leaderboard).
- **Why it fits:** Removes the anxiety-driven, performative "don't break the chain" pressure while keeping the retention mechanic.
- **MAU impact:** Reintroduces a proven daily-return driver without the burnout that causes Snapchat streak fatigue and churn.

## 3. Algorithmic For You Page → **"Unfiltered Feed"** (from TikTok)

- **Original mechanic:** ML-ranked, infinite, engagement-optimized content firehose from strangers.
- **Remix:** A discovery feed limited to **friends-of-friends only**, shown in strict chronological order (no ranking algorithm), capped at a fixed daily batch rather than infinite scroll.
- **Why it fits:** Discovery without the black-box virality machine that rewards performance over authenticity.
- **MAU impact:** Gives new/lapsed users a reason to open the app even on light-posting days, without recreating TikTok's addictive infinite-scroll loop that BeReal's brand explicitly rejects.

## 4. Filters & AR Effects → **"Context Tags"** (from Snapchat/Instagram)

- **Original mechanic:** Visual filters that alter or beautify the photo itself.
- **Remix:** No visual alteration of the photo ever — but users can add one honest, factual context tag after capture ("running late," "bad hair day," "just woke up"). Purely textual, never image-altering.
- **Why it fits:** Preserves BeReal's unedited-photo promise while still giving users the *expressive* payoff filters provide.
- **MAU impact:** Small daily engagement lift (tagging takes 2 seconds) without reopening the door to curated self-presentation.

## 5. Close Friends List → **"Inner Circle"** (from Instagram)

- **Original mechanic:** A smaller, curated audience for more candid posts than the main feed.
- **Remix:** A standing Inner Circle (max ~8 people) who see a slightly more candid second angle or longer capture window — same one-shot, no-retake rule, just a smaller, safer audience.
- **Why it fits:** Instagram uses Close Friends to *increase* curation for a public audience by carving out a "real" backstage. BeReal already *is* the backstage — so this remix gives people a place to be even more unguarded, not more polished.
- **MAU impact:** Deepens engagement among high-trust friend groups, which are the users least likely to churn.

## 6. Duets/Stitch → **"Same Moment"** (from TikTok)

- **Original mechanic:** React to or build on another user's content, driving cross-pollination and virality.
- **Remix:** When two friends' BeReals land within the same few minutes, the app can optionally pair them into a **"Same Moment"** card — no editing, no remixing the original photo, just a side-by-side of two authentic captures that happened to coincide.
- **Why it fits:** Captures the social/collaborative spark of Duets without allowing anyone to edit, react over, or reframe someone else's raw photo.
- **MAU impact:** Creates a shareable, novel social object ("we posted at the same time!") that's a natural reason to screenshot and share outside the app — organic acquisition.

## 7. Likes & Public Metrics → **"RealTalk Reactions"** (from Instagram/TikTok)

- **Original mechanic:** Public like/view counts that drive performance anxiety and content optimization for numbers.
- **Remix:** Keep BeReal's existing private, emoji-only reactions — but add one addition: a **private weekly digest** ("Maya reacted 😂 to 4 of your moments this week") sent only to the poster, never shown publicly or as a running counter.
- **Why it fits:** Gives users the warm feedback loop that reactions provide, with zero public scorekeeping to optimize against.
- **MAU impact:** The weekly digest is a built-in re-engagement notification that reliably brings lapsed posters back without incentivizing curation.

## 8. Explore/Discover Page → **"RealWorld Public"** (from Instagram/TikTok)

- **Original mechanic:** Algorithmically surfaced content from anywhere, optimized for time-on-app.
- **Remix:** Already scoped in the RealWorld concept — opt-in only, coarse-location discovery of public moments, no algorithmic ranking, just recency + proximity.
- **Why it fits:** Discovery without an engagement-maximizing algorithm shaping what gets seen.
- **MAU impact:** Gives BeReal a legitimate non-friend-graph reason to open the app, which is currently one of its biggest growth ceilings vs. Instagram/TikTok.

## 9. Profile Grid/Highlights → **"Memory Wall"** (from Instagram)

- **Original mechanic:** A curated, editable grid presenting an idealized version of someone's life.
- **Remix:** A profile view of your own past BeReals — but **uneditable, unremovable, and unsorted** (chronological only, no pinning your "best" posts, no deleting the unflattering ones except via the existing one-post-at-a-time deletion).
- **Why it fits:** Keeps the personal-archive payoff of a profile grid while refusing the core curation behavior (picking your best 9 photos) that makes Instagram grids performative.
- **MAU impact:** Personal archive value compounds over time (same mechanic as Footprints/Memories), raising switching cost and driving return visits for nostalgia, not performance.

## 10. Live/Broadcast → **"Group BeReal"** (from Instagram/TikTok Live)

- **Original mechanic:** Real-time broadcast to an audience, often performative and produced.
- **Remix:** When multiple friends are physically together, one shutter moment captures **everyone in the group simultaneously** (each person's own front/back camera, synced into one combined post) — still one shot, no retakes, no live audience or comments during capture.
- **Why it fits:** Gets the "something is happening right now" energy of Live without any performance-for-an-audience dynamic — it's still a single authentic capture, just a shared one.
- **MAU impact:** Strong viral/word-of-mouth driver — group posts naturally get shared outside the app (texted, storied elsewhere) as a novel format, and every participant has a reason to open BeReal in that moment.

---

## Summary: What Was Deliberately Left Out

To stay consistent with the brief, these common growth levers were **not** remixed in, because they're structurally incompatible with authenticity/minimalism even in a diluted form:

- **Beauty filters or any image editing** — conflicts with BeReal's core unedited-photo promise
- **Public follower/like counts** — inherently performance-optimizing
- **Infinite algorithmic scroll** — optimizes for time-on-app over authentic connection
- **Post scheduling or multi-take retries** — undoes the one-shot, real-time premise entirely
