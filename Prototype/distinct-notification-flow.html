Slotted into BeReal's existing Notifications screen as one new row under Posts — right next to "Friends' Posts," since it's the same category of alert. No new settings section, no separate menu. During the A/B test this stays fixed (not user-editable); it only becomes a real, user-facing toggle after rollout, as an opt-out safety valve.

⬇
Why this design works: the fix isn't about saying something new — it's about signaling this one is different before the user even reads it. A distinct color/glow, a live countdown instead of static copy, and a unique sound work together so the notification is recognized in the half-second glance most people give a lock screen, rather than relying on them to actually read and compare every alert.
