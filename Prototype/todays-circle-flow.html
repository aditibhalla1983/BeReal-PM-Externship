Today's Circle — How it appears, works, and is stored

A full-circle snapshot of friend posting activity, shown right after you post

1. It appearsRight after you post
✓ Your BeReal is shared

Right after you post, the confirmation screen shows Today's Circle — a ring across your whole friend list (not just close friends), so the moment right after posting has something to look at instead of just closing.

2. You exploreTap to browse

Tapping the ring expands into a dot grid — one dot per friend, filled if they've posted. Tapping any filled dot jumps straight into their moment, turning the "post and leave" moment into a few extra minutes of browsing.

3. It updatesRevisit anytime
17/20friends posted today

The ring is also reachable anytime from the Today tab, filling in live as more friends post throughout the day — a small reason to check back later without a push notification.

How it's storedData model

Same underlying data as Friend Check-In — a read view over the existing "posted today" flag — just aggregated differently.

Source dataexisting per-friend posted-today flag (same flag Friend Check-In reads)
Scopeyour full friend list (not just Close Friends — that's the main difference from Friend Check-In)
QuerySELECT COUNT(posted_today) / COUNT(*) FROM friends WHERE user = me
Persistencenone (recomputed on view, resets at the daily cycle)

Just as fast to build as Friend Check-In — same data source, no new backend. The only real work is the ring/progress UI itself.

Heads up — overlap with Friend Check-In: both features are built on the exact same "posted today" data, so if both ship, they should probably be one feature with two views rather than two separate builds: Friend Check-In = persistent strip, Close Friends only, always visible at the top of the home screen; Today's Circle = full friend list, shown as a moment right after posting rather than a persistent element. Worth deciding whether you want both, or to merge them into a single "who's posted" feature with two entry points.
