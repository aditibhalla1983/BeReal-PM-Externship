How it's storedData model

This feature needs no new stored data at all — it's a scheduled query over comments that already exist, surfaced as a transient in-app banner.

Trigger checkon app open (client-side query, not a push job)
Query logiccomments WHERE post.owner = me AND replied = false AND age > 3h
Comment / replyexisting comment thread fields, unchanged
Banner statenot persisted (re-computed each open, dismiss just hides it for that session)
Capshow at most 1–2 reminders at a time (avoid turning into a notification feed)

Because it's a read-only query over data that already exists (comments, reply status, timestamps), there's nothing new to build on the backend — just the query and the banner UI. This is the lowest-effort idea on the list.

Why this design choice matters: the reminder is scoped tightly — only your own unanswered comments, capped at 1–2 at a time, cleared automatically once viewed. That keeps it a gentle nudge rather than turning into another notification feed to manage, which would work against BeReal's low-pressure positioning.
