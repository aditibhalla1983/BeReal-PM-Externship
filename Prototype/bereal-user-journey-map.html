User Journey Map for BeReal User

From discovery to daily return — objectives, needs, touchpoints, pain points & opportunities at each stage

Discovery & Research of Platform Downloading & Onboarding First-Time Setup (Adding Friends) Daily Notification Capturing the Post Posting & Sharing Viewing Friends Reacting / Replying Revisiting Memories Returning Tomorrow
Objectives
Learn what BeReal is and whether it fits how they connect with friends
Get the app installed and account created with minimal friction
Build a friend list to make the app immediately useful
Be prompted at a good moment to capture something real
Take one authentic, unedited photo within the time window
Share the moment with friends without overthinking it
See what friends are doing right now
Respond authentically to a friend's moment
Reconnect with past authentic moments
Feel motivated to open and post again the next day
Need
Understand the app's core concept before committing
A fast, simple signup that doesn't ask for too much upfront
I should be able to add all my friends through every channel — contacts, social media, QR code, or shared links
A trigger that feels timely, not disruptive
Fast camera access with minimal steps between notification and capture
Confidence that the post is authentic and low-stakes
Easy access to friends' moments without needing to be asked
A low-effort way to show engagement
A reason to return to the app beyond the daily post
A reason to return that isn't just guilt or habit
Touchpoints
App Store listing, word-of-mouth from friends, social media mentions
App Store download, phone number verification, account creation screen
Contact sync prompt, friend search, friend requests
Push notification, lock screen alert
Dual camera capture screen, front/back camera
Post confirmation screen, caption/tag options
Friends feed, unlocked after posting
RealMoji reactions, comments
Memories tab, on-this-day surfacing
Next day's notification, streak status, friend activity
Pain Points
Confusing positioning vs. Instagram/Snapchat; unclear what makes it different
Asked for full contact list access with no selective/limited option — raises privacy concerns before the user has even seen the app's value
None — able to add friends smoothly across channels
Arrives at an inconvenient time (meeting, driving); users feel pressured or forget by the time they're free
Self-consciousness about appearance in an unposed, unedited photo — even with a flexible posting window, the pressure of "no filter, no retake" can make people hesitate to capture or share
Post feels like a dead end once shared — nothing else to do
Feed feels sparse if few friends posted; posting first to unlock can feel transactional
Conversations often end after one reaction/comment; feels shallow
Memories are easy to forget exist; buried in navigation
Streak pressure or guilt from missed days causes drop-off; no clear payoff for consistency
Opportunities
Clearer store listing copy highlighting the "one authentic moment" differentiation — e.g. tagline: "Capture your unfiltered memory."
Prompt users to choose full or limited contact access at onboarding, reducing privacy-driven churn before signup completes
None
Learn each user's typical active hours from past posting times, then send the daily notification within a randomized window inside that range — reducing missed/bad-timing notifications while keeping the moment spontaneous
If a friend posts around the same time, offer to link the two posts into one shared moment — shifts the focus from "how do I look" to "we shared this," easing self-consciousness
Post-Then-Explore — give the moment right after posting a small purpose
Quiet Digest / Passive Presence Widget — surface friend activity without requiring a post first
Reply prompts / Daily Question to extend a reaction into a real conversation
Auto-surface "On This Day" / Memory Throwback instead of requiring users to seek it out
Non-punitive Friend Streaks / "You Were Missed" moment to re-engage without shame
